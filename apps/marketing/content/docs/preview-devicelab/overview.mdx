# LiveLab & DeviceLab

Caide features a built-in preview stage in the Right Dock:
- **DeviceLab Frames**: Test your app inside realistic iPhone 16, Google Pixel, iPad, or Desktop viewports.
- **Console Log Stream**: Live stderr and stdout logs from your running Metro or Vite server.
- **Problems Tab**: Diagnostics and lint errors captured before commits.
